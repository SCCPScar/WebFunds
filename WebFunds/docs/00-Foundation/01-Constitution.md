# WebFunds Constitution

> **Every Euro Has a Story.**

---

# Purpose

The Constitution defines the immutable principles of WebFunds.

These principles are not implementation details. They are the foundation of every decision related to the product.

Whenever a conflict exists between features, technical constraints, or future ideas, this document always has priority.

---

# Scope

This document applies to:

- Product decisions
- User Experience
- Visual Design
- Artificial Intelligence
- Engineering
- Security
- Future Features

Nothing in the project may intentionally violate these principles.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. The User Is Always in Control

The owner makes every financial decision.

WebFunds may recommend.

WebFunds may automate repetitive tasks.

WebFunds never decides for the owner.

---

## 2. AI Assists, Never Controls

Artificial Intelligence exists to reduce effort.

It must never:

- move money
- delete information
- confirm transactions
- classify salaries automatically
- start Financial Cycles
- perform irreversible actions

Every important decision requires explicit user confirmation.

---

## 3. Every Euro Has a Story

Money is more than numbers.

Every transaction represents a real-life event.

Whenever possible, WebFunds should preserve:

- context
- memories
- receipts
- merchant information
- notes
- locations
- related people (future)

Understanding is more valuable than recording.

---

## 4. Financial Calm Comes First

Money often creates anxiety.

WebFunds should have the opposite effect.

The interface should reduce uncertainty.

Never create fear.

Never shame the user.

Never pressure decisions.

---

## 5. Simplicity Is a Requirement

Simple does not mean limited.

Simple means understandable.

Complexity belongs inside the software.

Never inside the interface.

---

## 6. Progressive Disclosure

Information should appear gradually.

Show only what is necessary.

Advanced details remain available,

but never overwhelm the user.

---

## 7. Trust Is Earned

Every automation must be transparent.

Every suggestion must be explainable.

Every important action must be reversible whenever possible.

Trust is built through predictability.

---

## 8. Privacy Is Fundamental

Financial information belongs exclusively to its owner.

Collect only the information required.

Store only what is necessary.

Protect everything.

Privacy is a product feature.

---

## 9. Security Without Friction

Security should protect.

Not interrupt.

Whenever possible, authentication should feel effortless through technologies such as Face ID, biometrics and secure sessions.

---

## 10. Consistency Creates Confidence

Buttons behave consistently.

Cards behave consistently.

Animations behave consistently.

Language remains consistent.

Predictability increases confidence.

---

## 11. Mobile First

The iPhone experience defines the product.

Desktop expands the experience.

Desktop never defines it.

---

## 12. Technology Exists to Serve the Experience

The desired experience determines the technology.

Never choose technology simply because it is popular.

Every technical decision must improve the product.

---

## 13. Replaceability

External services must always be replaceable.

Examples:

- AI providers
- OCR providers
- Banking providers
- Notification providers

No implementation should permanently lock the project to a single vendor.

---

## 14. Performance Is Part of Quality

Fast software feels trustworthy.

Every screen should feel immediate.

Performance is not an optimization.

It is a requirement.

---

## 15. Accessibility Is Mandatory

WebFunds should be usable by as many people as possible.

Accessibility is considered part of product quality.

Not an optional enhancement.

---

## 16. Financial Cycles Are Intentional

Time inside WebFunds is organized using Financial Cycles.

A Financial Cycle begins only when the owner decides.

Incoming money never starts a new cycle automatically.

---

## 17. Weaver Is a Companion

Weaver is not a chatbot.

Weaver is a financial companion.

Its purpose is to explain.

Guide.

Remember.

Suggest.

Never judge.

---

## 18. Every Screen Must Reduce Uncertainty

When leaving any screen,

the owner should understand more than before opening it.

If uncertainty increases,

the design has failed.

---

## 19. Product Quality Over Feature Quantity

More features do not necessarily create a better product.

Clarity.

Reliability.

Trust.

Maintainability.

These always have priority.

---

## 20. Long-Term Thinking

Every decision should remain valid for years.

Short-term convenience must never compromise long-term quality.

---

# Final Principle

Whenever uncertainty exists during design or development,

choose the solution that:

- increases clarity;
- respects the owner's control;
- preserves trust;
- simplifies the experience;
- and helps tell the story behind every Euro.
