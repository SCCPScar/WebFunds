# Domain Rules

> Every rule exists to protect financial integrity.

---

# RULE-DOM-001

Financial data is immutable.

Changes create new versions.

Nothing important is overwritten.

Priority:

Critical

---

# RULE-DOM-002

Every Transaction belongs to exactly one Financial Cycle.

Priority:

Critical

---

# RULE-DOM-003

A Financial Cycle can have only one Active status.

Priority:

Critical

---

# RULE-DOM-004

Dreams never modify bank balances.

Priority:

Critical

---

# RULE-DOM-005

Reserved Money always belongs to the owner.

Priority:

Critical

---

# RULE-DOM-006

Weaver never performs financial actions automatically.

Priority:

Critical

---

# RULE-DOM-007

Every AI suggestion includes confidence.

Priority:

Critical

---

# RULE-DOM-008

Every AI suggestion must explain its reasoning.

Priority:

High

---

# RULE-DOM-009

Mysteries are historical records.

Resolved Mysteries are never deleted.

Priority:

High

---

# RULE-DOM-010

Every Receipt belongs to one Transaction.

Priority:

High

---

# RULE-DOM-011

Every modification generates an Audit Event.

Priority:

Critical

---

# RULE-DOM-012

Manual data always has priority over AI suggestions.

Priority:

Critical

---

# RULE-DOM-013

Synchronization never destroys local history.

Priority:

Critical

---

# RULE-DOM-014

Every Report must be reproducible.

Priority:

High

---

# RULE-DOM-015

Offline behavior must preserve data integrity.

Priority:

Critical

---

# RULE-DOM-016

Every Notification must provide value.

Priority:

Medium

---

# RULE-DOM-017

The owner is always in control.

Priority:

Critical

---

# RULE-DOM-018

The application never judges financial decisions.

Priority:

Critical

---

# RULE-DOM-019

Every Euro can be traced.

Priority:

Critical

---

# RULE-DOM-020

Transparency always has priority over automation.

Priority:

Critical

---

# Final Principle

The integrity of financial history is the foundation of WebFunds.
