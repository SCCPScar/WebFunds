# Subscriptions

> Small recurring expenses deserve attention.

---

# Purpose

Subscriptions represent recurring payments detected from transaction history.

Subscriptions may be automatically suggested but are always confirmed by the owner.

---

# Core Principles

Subscriptions are inferred.

Never assumed.

Every suggestion includes confidence.

---
