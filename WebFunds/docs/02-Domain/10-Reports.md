# Reports

> Data becomes knowledge when it tells a story.

---

# Purpose

Reports transform raw financial data into meaningful insights.

Their objective is not only to present numbers, but to help the owner understand financial behavior over time.

---

# Core Principles

Reports never modify data.

Reports are generated from historical information.

Every number must be traceable.

Generated insights must explain their reasoning.
