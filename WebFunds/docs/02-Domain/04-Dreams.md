# Dreams

> Every dream begins with one reserved Euro.

---

# Purpose

Dreams represent financial objectives that the owner wants to achieve.

Unlike traditional budgeting applications, Dreams are not simply savings goals.

A Dream represents an intention.

Money reserved for a Dream has a purpose.

---

# Philosophy

Saving money should feel motivating.

Dreams transform future purchases into visible progress.

Every contribution should make the owner feel closer to something meaningful.

---

# Definition

A Dream is a financial objective with:

A target amount

Optional target date

Reserved balance

Progress

History

Predictions

Insights

---

# Core Principles

Dreams never move money automatically.

The owner decides every contribution.

Reserved money remains part of the owner's assets.

Dreams never modify bank balances.

---

# Draft

Created but not started.

No money reserved.

---

# Active

Receiving contributions.

Visible throughout the application.

Included in reports.

---

# Completed

Target amount reached.

Completion date recorded.

History preserved permanently.

---

# Archived

Read-only.

Historical reference.

Cannot receive new contributions.

---

# Cancelled

Stops accepting contributions.

Reserved money returns to Available Balance.

History is preserved.
