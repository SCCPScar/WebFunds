# Vault

> Money with a purpose.

---

# Purpose

Vault is the place where the owner reserves money for future goals, emergencies and planned expenses.

Unlike a traditional savings account, Vault represents intentions rather than bank accounts.

Money inside Vault is still the owner's money.

It is simply protected from impulsive spending.

---

# Philosophy

Available money answers:

"What can I spend today?"

Vault answers:

"What am I protecting for tomorrow?"

---

# Primary Goals

The owner should be able to:

• Create savings goals

• Reserve money

• Track progress

• Move money between goals

• Release reserved money

• Visualize future plans
